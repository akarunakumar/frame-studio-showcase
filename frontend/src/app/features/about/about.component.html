About us

Interiors built around how you live, not a template.

Frame Studio Interiors designs and executes residential and commercial spaces with a focus on functionality, craftsmanship, and quality — from a single room to a complete home.

Our design philosophy

Good interior design starts with a question, not a mood board: how will this room actually be used? We design around real routines — cooking, working, hosting, unwinding — and let the aesthetic follow from that, rather than the other way around.

The result is a space that looks considered because it is: every material, finish, and fixture earns its place.
